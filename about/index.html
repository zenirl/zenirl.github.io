About

Small games for a small screen.

ZenIRL makes games and apps for Wear OS watches. Each one is designed for the round screen from the start, not ported from a phone.

How we build

  • Crown first.The rotating crown (or bezel) is the main control, so you can play without covering the screen.
  • Haptics are gameplay.In games like Crown Lockpick, Safecracker and Haptic Beat, you play by feel.
  • Round by design.No corners. The HUD hugs the bezel so the game gets the whole circle.
  • Standalone and offline.No phone needed, no account, no internet required.
  • No ads, no IAP.Every game is free. Nothing to unlock, nothing to buy.
  • No tracking.No analytics SDKs, no data collected. Privacy policies are linked from each game page.

Part of Helpivy

ZenIRL is the watch-games label of Helpivy. Helpivy also makes free, ad-free learning games and apps for kids aged 4 and up, including Painti (Punjabi) and Bolo (Hindi). Same rules on both sides: free, no ads, no tracking.
