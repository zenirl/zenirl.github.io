👶 Sitter Log

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Summary

Sitter Log is a babysitter and nanny handoff app. It is local-first — children's profiles, daily activity, photos, inventory, and PDFs stay on the phone running the app. The app does not include analytics or crash reporting.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Children profiles (name, date of birth, allergies, doctor/vet, emergency contact, behaviour notes)
  • Activity logs (feedings, walks, naps, medications, notes)
  • Photos
  • Inventory (formula, diapers, medications)
  • Reminders

Stored in the app's private Room database (sitter.db) and private file directories. Included in Android system backup (Google Drive) if you have that on. We never see this data.

Live Share with Parent (Opt-In)

If you enable the "Share with parent" flow, the app:

  • Signs you in anonymously to Firebase Authentication (no email or password).
  • Creates a short share code that the parent opens in a browser to see the day's activity in real time and acknowledge it.
  • Uploads child summary data + day activity + parent check-ins to a private Firestore document gated by the share code. The share auto-expires.

Photos and the child's full profile do not leave the device through this flow — only summary text and counts. Toggle share off and no further syncs happen.

Backup goes to the Firebase project sitter-zenirl. We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising.

Sharing PDFs

When you generate a day summary or sitter brief PDF and tap share, the file is written to the app's private cache and handed to the Android share sheet (WhatsApp, email, etc.). Nothing uploads through us in that flow.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification beyond the anonymous Firebase user ID
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Photos (these never leave the device)

Children's Privacy

Sitter Log is a tool used by adults (parents, sitters, nannies) to manage care for children. Children do not interact with the app directly. All decisions about what information to enter are made by the caregiving adult.
