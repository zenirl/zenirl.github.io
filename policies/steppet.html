🐾 Step Pet

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Summary

Step Pet stays on your watch. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads. The app has no internet permission and cannot make any network call. Your step count is read on the device and used only to feed and grow your pet — it never leaves the watch.

Step & Activity Data

With your permission, Step Pet reads your daily step count from the watch's step sensor via Android's Activity Recognition and Health Services. Steps are used entirely on-device to award coins, trigger adventures, and grow your pet. Step Pet does not store a step history, share your steps, or transmit them anywhere.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Your pet (name, species, growth stage, mood, bond, coins, items owned, adventures, streak)
  • Pets you've raised to adult (the "family")
  • Today's step snapshot and your chosen daily goal

Stored in the app's private DataStore (steppet). Included in Android system backup (Google Drive) only if you have backup enabled.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Step Pet's background worker may post at most one local notification per day — e.g. a gentle nudge to take a walk, or that your pet returned from an adventure. The check runs entirely on your device.

Permissions

  • Physical activity (Activity Recognition) — to count your steps
  • Notifications — for the optional daily nudge
  • Vibrate — for tap and celebration haptics
  • Wake lock — so the background step tick can run briefly

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Any data sent off the device — there is no network access

Children's Privacy

Step Pet is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal data. It does not target users under 13 with data collection of any kind.
