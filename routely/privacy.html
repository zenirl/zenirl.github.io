🌿 Routely

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Summary

Routely is a route-based service tracker for solo operators (lawn care, pool service, handyman, pest control, etc.). It is local-first — your customers, properties, schedules, jobs, photos, and signatures live on your phone only. No analytics, no crash reporter, no cloud backend.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Customers (name, phone, email, notes)
  • Properties (address, gate code, notes)
  • Service schedules (frequency, rate, next date)
  • Jobs (services performed, materials, worker notes, start/end timestamps)
  • Before / after photos
  • Customer signatures (PNG)
  • Business profile (name, phone, email, address, currency, UPI ID)

Stored in the app's private Room database (routely.db) and private file directories for photos and signatures. Included in Android system backup (Google Drive) if you have that turned on.

Map Links

Tapping Open in Maps launches your default maps app with the property address via a geo: deep link. The map provider sees the address. We do not see anything.

Sharing PDFs and CSV

When you generate a job report PDF or export the jobs CSV, the file is written to the app's private cache and handed to the Android share sheet (WhatsApp, email, Drive, etc.) for the app you pick. Nothing uploads through us.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the morning reminder worker checks the day's planned jobs on-device and posts a single local notification. No data leaves the device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification
  • Real-time location (we never request location permission)
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Photos or signatures (these stay on the device)

Children's Privacy

Routely is a business tool intended for adults. It does not target users under 13.
