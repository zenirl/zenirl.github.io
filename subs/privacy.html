Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Summary

Subly stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads. The app has no internet permission and cannot make any network call.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Subscriptions you add (name, amount, currency, billing cycle, next renewal date, category, color, notes, cancel URL)
  • Settings (default currency, default reminder lead days)

Stored in the app's private Room database (subly.db) and SharedPreferences. Included in Android system backup (Google Drive) if you have it on.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Subly's background worker checks daily for upcoming renewals and posts a local notification N days before each one. The check runs entirely on your device.

Sharing CSV

When you tap Export CSV, a file is written to the app's private cache and handed to the Android share sheet for an app you pick (Drive, email, etc.). Nothing uploads through us.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Cancel URLs or payment details — those just open in your browser when tapped

Children's Privacy

Subly is intended for adults managing their own subscriptions. It does not target users under 13.
