🌙 Cycle

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Your data stays on your phone. We can't see it because there is no server.

Cycle has no internet permission, no account, no cloud, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs that phone home.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Cycles (start date, end date, notes)
  • Daily logs (flow, symptoms, mood, temperature, sexual-activity flag, notes)
  • Settings (average cycle override, reminder lead days, PIN hash + salt)

Stored in the app's private Room database (cycle.db) and SharedPreferences. The database and preferences are explicitly excluded from Android cloud backup — they only travel between your devices via direct device-transfer.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, a daily background worker checks predictions on-device and posts a local notification when your period is predicted in N days or on the predicted ovulation day. No data leaves the device.

PIN Lock

If you set a PIN, it is hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (100,000 iterations, random 16-byte salt) before being stored. The hash and salt are kept in the app's private SharedPreferences. The plaintext PIN is never stored.

Sharing CSV

When you tap Export CSV, the file is written to the app's private cache and handed to the Android share sheet for an app you pick. Nothing uploads through us.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Cycle data, symptoms, mood, or temperature data — these never leave your device
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Crash reports

Why This Matters

Cycle data is sensitive. We built Cycle so that there is no third party — not us, not an advertiser, not a data broker — who can see your health information, because the data physically does not travel anywhere outside your phone.

Children's Privacy

Cycle is intended for menstruating adolescents and adults tracking their own bodies. We do not collect any data, so there is no data collection to consent to.
